# Aiesharr Ecosystem

Aiesharr is the project trunk for a creator-owned education, media, AI, and social commerce ecosystem.

This project exists to turn Amber Wilder's connected interests into one buildable digital village: Freedom School, living art, music, family education, AI workflows, marketplace tools, and future Web3 ownership layers.

## Mission

Build a practical ecosystem that helps people move from survival mode into ownership, intelligence, creativity, and legacy through education, media, automation, and community.

## First-Year Vision

Aiesharr will begin as a simple web platform and grow into a connected ecosystem.

The first year focuses on:

1. Brand foundation
2. Landing page and email capture
3. Course/resource library
4. Digital product marketplace
5. Music and living art showcase
6. Community waitlist
7. AI-assisted workflow layer
8. Future Web3/NFT ownership experiments

## Main Pillars

### Freedom School
Education for what people were not taught: emotional intelligence, financial literacy, AI literacy, family systems, ownership, and creator economy basics.

### Suppressedflowerz / Scroll Chambers
Music, storytelling, living art, atmosphere, and cultural media that bring the ecosystem to life.

### Aiesharr Tech Layer
AI workflows, automation, dashboards, Codex tasks, project memory, and tools that make the ecosystem easier to run.

### Marketplace
Digital products, templates, workbooks, sound packs, course kits, art assets, and future token-gated ownership experiments.

### Community
A safe entry point for builders, families, creators, learners, and people rebuilding their lives.

## MVP Build

The first version should be a simple website, not a complicated blockchain build.

MVP features:

- Homepage
- Mission section
- Ecosystem pillars
- Email signup/waitlist
- Course/resource preview
- Music/media links
- Product preview section
- Community invitation

## Suggested Tech Stack

- Next.js or Vite + React
- Tailwind CSS
- Markdown or JSON content files
- Supabase later for accounts/database
- Stripe later for products/payments
- Vercel or Cloudflare Pages for deployment
